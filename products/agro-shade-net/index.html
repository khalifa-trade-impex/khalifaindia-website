Agro Shade Net

Khalifa Trade & Impex is an Ahmedabad-based manufacturer of HDPE Agro Shade Net, with around 5 years of in-house manufacturing experience. Our own brand, SKYGUARD™ AGRO SHADE NET, is manufactured using HDPE material with a focus on consistent quality, strength and reliable performance.

RELATIONSHIP — Own Manufacturing
Product range

SKYGUARD™ AGRO SHADE NET

We manufacture shade nets in 9 Gauge and 12 Gauge specifications, including 75% and 90% shade options.

Border options
Mono Border Black Border Yellow Border

Supplied as per requirement.

Sizes

Standard sizes are available, while customised sizes can also be prepared as required. Common sizes such as 16×32, 16×64 and 32×64 can be prepared, with customised sizing and finishing options available as required.

Supply

Bundles & supply

We can supply shade net in different bundle formats, including approximately 40–45 kg bundles and 73 kg bundles, along with customised piece-size requirements.

Reach

We supply across India and cater to bulk as well as customised requirements.
