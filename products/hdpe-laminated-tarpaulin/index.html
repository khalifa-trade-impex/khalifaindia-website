HDPE Laminated Tarpaulin

Khalifa Trade & Impex is an Ahmedabad-based manufacturer of HDPE Laminated Tarpaulin, with more than 10 years of manufacturing experience. Our own brand, BADSHAH TARPAULIN, is manufactured with a focus on consistent quality, strength and dependable performance.
