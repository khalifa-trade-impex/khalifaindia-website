About us

A B2B supply business built on three clear capabilities

Khalifa Trade & Impex is an Ahmedabad-based business engaged in manufacturing, authorised channel partnerships, sourcing and supply of industrial, agricultural, plumbing and infrastructure products.

Who We Are

Khalifa Trade & Impex is an Ahmedabad-based B2B business engaged in manufacturing, authorised channel partnerships, sourcing and supply of industrial, agricultural, plumbing and infrastructure products. Our registered office is at Dariyapur, Ahmedabad, with our factory at Saijpur-Gopalpur, Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

What We Do

We work across three clearly separated capabilities, and every product in our catalogue is labelled with the relationship it actually carries.

  • Own manufacturing
  • Authorised brand partnerships
  • Sourcing & supply network

Our Manufacturing

We manufacture HDPE laminated tarpaulin under our own brand, BADSHAH TARPAULIN, along with agro shade net under our own brand, SKYGUARD™. Quality, grade, GSM and size options are offered against customer requirement.

  • HDPE Laminated Tarpaulin
  • Agro Shade Net
  • BADSHAH TARPAULIN — Built strong. Made to protect.
  • SKYGUARD™ — Shade for growing conditions.

Our Authorised Partnerships

We are an Authorised Channel Partner of EPP Composites Pvt. Ltd. for Ahmedabad district and surrounding areas, and an Authorised Channel Partner for CORSA PTMT Bath Fittings and for Teraflo HDPE Pipes & Fittings, both for Ahmedabad. These are partnerships — we do not describe ourselves as the distributor of any principal.

  • EPP Composites Pvt. Ltd. — Authorised Channel Partner
  • CORSA PTMT Bath Fittings — Authorised Channel Partner
  • Teraflo HDPE Pipes & Fittings — Authorised Channel Partner

Our Sourcing & Supply Network

Beyond manufacturing and authorised partnerships, we source and supply products from established manufacturers, dealers, distributors and suppliers according to customer requirement, on an order-to-order basis. These products are shown as Sourced & Supplied.

Our Approach

We keep our positioning accurate, our catalogue organised, and our responses quick. Requirements are quoted on enquiry, with product data confirmed before it is published.

Manufacturer • Authorised Channel Partner • Supplier

Manufacturing Strength. Authorised Partnerships. Reliable Supply.
